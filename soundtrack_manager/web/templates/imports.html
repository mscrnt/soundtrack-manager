How it works
  1. Check reads the file and reports problems without importing anything.
  2. Import searches the site for each entry and scores the results against the full title. Generic words (“Original Soundtrack”, “OST”) don't count, numbers must agree, and a year or platform in the title, e.g. (1996) or (NES), helps.
  3. A clear winner is matched automatically and its album page is checked for the format you want. Close calls wait for you under Needs review.
  4. Queue sends the matches to Downloads in priority order.

One site search per entry, at the normal request pace, so a 500-entry list takes a while. Matching continues after a restart.
